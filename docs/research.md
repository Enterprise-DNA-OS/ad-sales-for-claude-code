# Selection and source notes

Checked 17 October 2026. New family: advertising-sales, publisher-side commercial orders. Existing media-planning is the buyer-side planning family. No surveys or telecom service operations are included.

| Candidate | Bill basis | Six scores: bill, data, buyers, search, build, import | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Salesforce Media Cloud | US$54,000 for ten Advanced seats at US$450 per month, annual contract | 4, 5, 2, 2, 5, 5 | 23 |
| Salesforce Education Cloud admissions | US$20,880 for twenty nonprofit Core seats at US$87 per month, annual contract | 3, 5, 2, 2, 4, 5 | 21 |
| Envoy Visitors | US$21,720 for five Premium locations at US$362 per month, annual billing, before platform fees | 3, 3, 2, 2, 3, 5 | 18 |

These are illustrative list-price scenarios, not verified customer bills. Education's nonprofit pricing does not establish for-profit pricing. Envoy's kiosk and physical arrival workflow make a purely internal desk a weaker fit. No source demonstrated measured alternative-search volume, so search scores are conservative. The available internal CRM/customer snapshots did not establish a named buyer or invoice for these tools. No live customer system was accessed. Buyer scores reflect that evidence gap, not a claim that a known client pays the bill.

Primary sources:

- [Media Cloud pricing](https://www.salesforce.com/media/cloud/pricing/): Advanced US$450/user/month, annual billing. Advertising Sales Management, Revenue Cloud Advanced, contracts and orders are listed. Partner integration products and audience-related add-ons are separate purchasing decisions.
- [Media Cloud data model](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=ind.media_ad_sales_management_learn_data_model.htm&language=en_US&type=5): advertiser/agency, placement and media-plan relationships. No single universal CSV is promised.
- [Salesforce export walkthrough](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/projects/create-reports-and-dashboards-for-sales-and-marketing-managers/manage-reported-data): Details Only CSV and Unicode UTF-8.
- [Education pricing](https://www.salesforce.com/education/pricing/): nonprofit Core US$87/user/month, annual billing; not a registration in this run.
- [Envoy pricing](https://envoy.com/pricing): Visitors Premium US$362/location/month, annual billing; not a registration in this run.
- [NZ Privacy Principle 8](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/8/), [Principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/) and [ASA code](https://asa.co.nz/codes/codes/advertising-standards-code/): human evidence and record review prompts. See compliance.md for the limits and 2027 transition.

No claim is made that Salesforce cannot report these questions or that the free desk replaces every feature. It replaces a bounded internal workflow. Prices exclude implementation and do not imply a guaranteed saving. Demo names, orders and amounts are invented fixtures explicitly labelled as demo data; the page screens are captured from actual runs of those fixtures.
