# Evidence checks for an advertising sales desk

Checked 17 October 2026. The compliance command identifies record gaps. It does not inspect creative files, decide whether an advertisement is lawful or certify a publisher. Human review remains responsible for the content and evidence.

| Check | Source | Implementation and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Accuracy review for advertiser records | [NZ Privacy Principle 8](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/8/) | Missing review date or evidence reference creates a task. Booking and closure require a recorded review. A date does not establish that the information is accurate. |
| Retention review | [NZ Privacy Principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/) | A missing reason or expired review date creates a task. The next review date is a publisher policy, not a statutory retention period. Review and delete through an approved retention process; the CLI never automatically deletes personal information. |
| Creative and claims review | [NZ ASA Advertising Standards Code](https://asa.co.nz/codes/codes/advertising-standards-code/) | Each placement needs a reviewed creative version and substantiation reference before booking. If there are no objective claims, record the human review and that finding. The software stores references; it does not judge the evidence. |

The ASA page also announces an updated code for new advertising from 1 February 2027 and all advertising from 1 April 2027. Review the applicable code at that transition; this build makes no assertion of compliance with future wording. The workflow is an evidence checklist, not a hard-coded interpretation of every advertising rule. Specialist categories and jurisdictions require separate review.

Advertiser authority, one daily report per placement, accepted replacement impressions and evidenced closure are local commercial controls. They are not represented as statutory requirements. The demo review intervals are synthetic policy examples. No fixed legal retention period is encoded.

Do not store audience-level tracking data, payment details or raw sensitive creative content here. Original imported rows are part of the records to review for retention. Exports and local drafts also require access and retention controls.
